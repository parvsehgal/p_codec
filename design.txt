The Application is divided in 3 main classes

  1. the chroma class -> it handles the conversion of raw rgb24 files into yuv420 subsample and vice versa
  2. the dct class it is used to perform the discrete cosine transformation and quantization on 8x8 sections of the image
  3. the entropy class that is used to Huffman encode the transformed and quantized coefficients 

the above classes also have corresponding decode functions in them
the classes are combined for orchestration in the coder class
